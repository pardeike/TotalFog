# Total Fog

Fog of war and shared field of view for RimWorld. Explore the map using your
colonists, animals, cameras, watchtowers, and other vision providers. Configure
sight, hearing, fog appearance, and delayed alerts in the mod settings.

Enable Harmony and Total Fog. Disable other Real Fog of War versions.

Total Fog is an independent continuation of Real Fog of War by Luca De Petrillo
and its NWN continuation maintained by Mlie. Apache License 2.0 and the original
credits are preserved in LICENSE.md and NOTICE.md.
