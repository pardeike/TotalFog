You only know what your colonists can see.

Total Fog brings real fog of war to RimWorld. The map stays dark until someone looks. When your people walk away, the area turns grey and whatever moves there is gone from view. Raids come out of the dark. A noise at night might be a boar, or something much worse. If you want to know, you have to look.

What you get:

- Colonists, mechs, trained animals and turrets see around them, and walls block their view
- Explored land stays on the map in grey, but you only see who is there while someone watches
- Surveillance cameras, a watch telescope and a 3x3 watchtower for your lookouts
- Combat music no longer gives away a raid, and hidden sounds can be muted or muffled
- News about unseen events can wait until someone actually sees them
- Optional silent raids, and enemies that only shoot at what they can see
- Four settings pages, from light atmosphere to hard survival

Runs on RimWorld 1.6 and 1.7, needs Harmony, and speaks 14 languages. It replaces other Real Fog of War versions and takes over their explored maps, so turn those off and back up your save before switching.

Total Fog continues Real Fog of War by Luca De Petrillo and its NWN continuation by Mlie. Thank you both.
