# Total Fog artwork

## Current watchtower graphics

The player tower uses the accepted compact-deck design in
`Source/TotalFog/Watchtowers/WatchtowerGraphic.cs`. Two cached colored meshes
draw the body and foreground railing with native materials and stuff colors.
`WatchtowerWallJoins.cs` captures and clips actual adjacent walls' printed
geometry, retaining native materials/UVs and rebuilding on local wall changes.
The production tower registers wall-link flags across its solid 3x3 footprint.
No tower-body texture or custom shader/AssetBundle is required. The UI icons
below are separate PNGs.

Blueprints and placement ghosts use the native tint, strong outer contours,
translucent fill and broad translucent inner strokes. Wooden cross-braces are
omitted from previews. A four-rotation cache supplies the two preview passes.
The ladder defaults to the front on new placements; saved rotations stay intact.
Native 1.7 blueprint and tower follow-ups are recorded in VALIDATION.md.
Gameplay, projected occupant drawing and remaining acceptance belong to
[WATCHTOWER.md](WATCHTOWER.md#production-checkpoint).

## Historical watchtower artwork studies

The first 11 October geometry sketch is retained through the non-shipping
`Source/BridgeTools/WatchtowerVisualPrototype.cs`. It builds two tower meshes
once per spawn, uses RimWorld's native vertex-color material and stuff colors,
and draws native pawns with a scoped test-only projection. It adds no PNG,
shader or AssetBundle to the player payload. Wood/granite and native straight/
corner wall junctions were inspected in public 1.6 on 11 October 2026.
That draft kept the 3x3 base and tapered to a 1.4-cell deck, with broad
surface divisions and thicker dark outlines. The upper deck/railing is shifted
0.18 cells toward the viewer while the lower wall joins stay fixed;
the ladder's upper attachment follows the deck. The pawn has a separate
1.65-cell drawing projection, fitted so the body base sits near the deck center
instead of treating the sprite origin as its feet. One combined native screenshot
contains all eight material/occupancy/wall/rotation sketches. Earlier wide-deck
captures remain preserved in the validation ledger.
`WatchtowerWallJoins.cs` additionally caches clipped fragments generated by the
actual neighbouring walls' native Print calls. Their existing materials and
texture coordinates are retained; there is no new wall texture or masking shader.
The native straight wood/corner granite examples add one/two batched draw calls.
Andreas rejected the stretched wall-strip design; it remains recoverable at
commit 1e1dc54 and in the validation ledger. The previous colored-mesh artwork
is restored as the default, including its deck and broad surface divisions.
The separate `nativeFrontPieces` fixture option copies a real straight wall's
printed front band, translates/repeats it at native map scale and clips it to
the tower's front outline. Its original materials, UVs and vertex data remain;
there is no scaling, shearing or texture modification. Side faces and decks keep
the previous artwork. The combined experiment retains two unchanged controls.
The default needs two body/foreground draw calls plus cached wall joins; the
optional front adds one cached material pass in the native wood/granite examples.
Geometry is built before capture, with no texture lookup or rebuilding in DrawAt.
The crop still assumes the native wall-front band geometry. Different custom
wall shapes/shaders, styled-wall selection and native front-piece copying on
metallic towers remain unverified.
No third-party wall renderer or general performance acceptance is claimed.
The separate `silhouetteOutlines` fixture option retains two accepted controls
beside six outline studies. Outer support contours stay heavy; face boundaries,
braces and masonry details are lighter. Connected sections of the support contour
become thin seams while the actual native wall fragments remain above them.
Ladder uprights retain strong edges around their open spaces. This changes only
cached mesh geometry, with no extra material passes. Andreas's three original
building references then informed broad face shading, narrow light edges on
beams/rails, softer internal shadows and quieter masonry lines. The earlier
hairline study remains in the validation ledger; the refined edges are wider
and lower contrast. Native overview, straight wood join and granite corner
pixels were inspected. Andreas accepted the refined direction for further work;
this was still a non-shipping artwork prototype at that checkpoint.
The later `materialComparison` option uses that draft for ten labelled
towers: wood, steel, plasteel, sandstone, granite, limestone, slate, marble,
uranium and bioferrite. Each has real matching native walls on its left/right.
Gold/jade are excluded at Andreas's request; silver is omitted from this
practical comparison, not prohibited by a shipping definition. Tower mesh colours
now use `Thing.DrawColor`, matching the native wall's stuff colour handling.
Only timber has cross-braces/planked decks; masonry retains soft courses, and
metal has smooth faces/decks. All variants keep the wooden ladder.
Rounded joins fill the ends of heavy contours and beam strips in the current
study. These are cached vertices in the existing meshes, not new draw passes.
The comparison temporarily disables RimWorld's lighting overlay to show material
colours without dawn tint, then restores the setting. Native wall-front texture
copying remains the separate option; the tower's shaded faces are drawn geometry.
The supplied `all walls.png` remains an ignored visual reference. The old design
and earlier studies remain recoverable in Git and the validation ledger.
This is an artwork prototype, not the new tower's gameplay implementation;
see [WATCHTOWER.md](WATCHTOWER.md).
The access companion reuses the accepted meshes for a native ladder-job
experiment. Rungs sit below the pawn's drawing layer so a front-facing ladder
does not cross its body. At that checkpoint, timed movement read native job
progress while climbing occlusion still needed review. The production drawing
and gameplay now use the accepted meshes; the earlier controls remain history.

## Portable inherited textures

Original Real Fog of War gameplay textures and assets retain their upstream
Apache 2.0 attribution in NOTICE.md. The portable texture folder is loaded for
the current supported game version as well as the historical versions.
The active build loads ordinary PNGs from `LegacyAssets/Textures/`. It has no
custom AssetBundle or shader. The removed upstream bundle contained only
Texture2D assets duplicated by those PNG paths.

The public `upstream-baseline` tag preserves the original payload for comparison.
Baseline staging restores its AssetBundle and original load folders, then adds
the same `LegacyAssets` portable texture fallback used by the candidate. The
original DLL and definition bindings stay unchanged. Normal
staging removes any stale `Assets/` folder. Historical version payloads remain
frozen. Upstream scratch projects, failure experiments, duplicate translation
templates/ZIP and superseded branding binaries were removed from `Originals/`
on 7 October; they remain recoverable in Git history, with attribution retained.

## Watchtower UI icons

The new tower's Architect designator and native build-copy command use
`Current/Textures/UI/Watchtower/Build.png`. This is a neutral grayscale thumbnail
of the accepted compact deck, slanted solid base and front ladder. Its separate
`uiIconPath` leaves the procedural in-game building and blueprint graphics alone.
Existing watch, descend and retrieval float-menu actions use the matching tower,
ladder/down-arrow and carrying-person icons. All are ordinary transparent PNGs;
they add no shader, AssetBundle, per-frame drawing or gameplay state.

The icons were generated with OpenAI image generation on 2026-10-12.
`Build.png` is 256 by 256; `Descend.png` and `Retrieve.png` are 128 by 128.
The tower source was cropped to a centered 900 by 900 square at pixel offset
177,177, then resampled. The action icons were resampled without cropping.
Normalization used macOS `sips`, preserved transparency and did not stretch the
square source images. The original generated files remain in the local image
archive, outside the player payload. No legacy watchtower art was reused.
Native in-game icon appearance remains to be checked after installing the candidate.
